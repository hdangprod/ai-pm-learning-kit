## Five structured anchors

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
|Bounded and proportionate action|Full commitment without justification, or unpermitted content/contact|A direction without scope/effort cap|One task, reversible role-bounded plan, permission boundary; actual stage feasibility must also pass|
|Accurate scope of evidence|Causal, market, unique-need, person-count or executed-study overclaim|Useful evidence mentioned without unit/type|Trace corpus/source-audit units separately from simulation; keep need and learning effects unverified|
|Appropriate comparator|Skip incumbent because dedicated AI is presumed superior|Feature-only comparison|Suitable configured incumbent, same job, individual baseline, time/effort/access conditions|
|Missingness and uncertainty|Impute unknown outcomes as failure/success|Unspecified “more data”|Preserve unknown outcomes; audit coverage/opportunities and competing explanations|
|Explicit revision rule|Never revise, or automatically switch strategy|Revisit later without test/deadline|Owner/date/next check and measurable stop/narrow/revise conditions endorsed in the structured answer|

Each actual memo requires independent review. Selecting the2-point wording does not establish that learner-authored text implements it. Presence/date checks are transparent mechanical checks only. All critical endorsements block completion, including when the five dimension scores otherwise total10 and a context response ignores rights or invents missing outcomes.

## Evidence board key

E-AUDIT: directly observed **source-audit structure**, not learner behavior. E-REPORT: reported episode. E-INFERENCE: analyst implication/proposed comparison. E-ASSUMPTION: unmeasured effort prediction. E-SIM and E-ACTIVITY: simulation. E-MISSING: missing/unknown new-product delayed outcome. These categories describe evidence form; they are not a visual confidence ladder.

## Reassessment examples

**Capacity:**12→2 engineering hours makes listed A8h infeasible even though other staff have time. Keep A with a2h engineering reduced prototype and a correspondingly limited next check, or choose another bounded feasible plan. Keeping or changing can both pass; the keep/revise response must match the strategy actually selected. No credit merely for switching.

**Rights:** no participant contact/content this round, even if an email is offered. Keep the strategic direction with invented prompts and local protocol rehearsal, or choose a focused rights/definition audit. A later permission request does not authorize work now. Do not report hypothetical participation or outcomes as real.

**Coverage:**4/8 =50% assessed; four outcomes unknown. Audit response selection, task opportunities and measurement. Keeping or changing the strategy can be defensible, but a claim that the missing learners failed/succeeded is critical. This illustration is not execution of the original delayed study.

Record the original and revised memos separately, identify the fact and its decision relevance, and specify an updated or explicitly reaffirmed next action and measurable rule. Free-text content is stored without semantic certification.

## Transfer key — configured incumbent succeeds

Within the stipulated task/group, the incumbent meets the agreed delayed unaided outcome within the effort budget. That weakens the underserved claim for this job. It does not prove universal sufficiency or rule out other tasks, access problems, maintenance effort or learning needs.

B can maintain/rehearse the adequate workflow. D can target a specified residual uncertainty. C can investigate a support need in a defined residual task without assuming it exists. A can prepare a small residual-task prototype only as a bounded incremental hypothesis, after reconsidering whether bespoke investment is needed. Unconditional duplication based on complaint counts fails. “No new product can ever help anyone” also fails. Do not invent positive residual evidence; specify what would change the investment conclusion.

Transfer requires its own memo, five2-point checks, feasible plan and correct scoped context. Its solution gate follows a complete transfer attempt, independently of initial/revised consultation. No automatic reveal or award follows viewing guidance.

## Independent educational-quality audit checklist

A reviewer should inspect representative failed/passing memos, including A–D and justified keep/revise cases, against the original curriculum. Check learner understanding of evidence form, constructs, denominator/coverage, comparator adequacy, rights, role costs, actual measurability of the rule and whether transfer genuinely narrows the claim. Check the English adaptation with a Vietnamese-source reviewer. Evaluate cognitive load, hinting, option-order cues, accessibility and delayed unseen-case performance. Repeated selection of consistent “correct” wording is not evidence of mastery. An independent learner study would be required for an educational-effectiveness claim; none has been conducted.
