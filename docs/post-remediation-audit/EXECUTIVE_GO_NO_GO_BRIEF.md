## Why the recommendation is conditional

The review workflow now correctly prevents a structured 10/10 from being treated as a human-rated memo pass. Normal retries preserve first responses and require fresh records. However, an executed two-instance probe lost the earlier instance's saved first response after a stale whole-map write. Fault-injected malformed state could either crash rendering or allow a later edit to replace recoverable history. Those weaknesses matter when the product's history is used as study evidence.

The forms have correct reviewed arithmetic but uneven conceptual demands, wording/scoring ambiguities, a taught numerical example and no observed timing evidence. A first-person study can observe task performance and delayed application after these issues are controlled. It cannot attribute improvement causally to the app or compare unequated form totals as a validated growth scale.

## Scope and delivery

The seven reports provide the source/verification matrix, restricted form validation, worked reviewer calibration, deferred AQ05–AQ11 priorities and a learner-safe first-person protocol. Detailed audit/form/calibration material stays with moderators; give the learner only the readiness plan and their assigned form. No participants, human ratings, learner outcomes or repository changes were produced during this audit.
