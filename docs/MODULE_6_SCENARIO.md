## Rubric and valid decisions (locked)

Exactly five dimensions: bounded/proportionate action; accurate evidence scope; appropriate comparator; missingness/uncertainty; explicit revision rule. Each structured response scores0,1,2; total0–10. Missing/out-of-range responses score0 and remain incomplete. A generic but noncritical response scores1. A fully specified defensible response scores2. All five must score2 (10/10), the selected plan must fit actual stage resource caps, rights must be respected, required text fields must be present and dates valid, and stage-specific checks must pass. This preserves the existing exercise specification: choosing A/B/C/D itself earns no points. No reward is given just for changing strategy.

Each of A–D, listed or reduced, is acceptable initially under its declared bounded scope and resource conditions. A is a prototype to investigate incremental value, B checks a configured incumbent, C investigates necessary human support, and D answers a specified decision-relevant uncertainty. None grants rollout or validated need.

Critical zero-point options explicitly flag: observational weekly activity as causal learning proof; simulated analytics as real product-market evidence; secondary research as unique unmet-need validation; ignoring data rights; missing outcomes as confirmed failure OR success; and a proposed experiment as executed. Any critical endorsement blocks passing regardless of other dimensions. These deterministic rules assess selected statements; memo text is presence-checked, not semantically certified. An independent reviewer must assess whether the actual memo is coherent and proportionate.

Full instructor anchors/examples appear only after a submitted attempt with all structured groups selected and all required text present. Correctness is not required for reveal. Revealing never awards completion and consultation history remains sticky. Scores are structured practice checks, not the six-week plan's separate0–8 operating rubric, the Volume II0–20 final, professional certification or measured mastery.

## Memo and immutable records

Initial/revised memo fields: decision; supporting evidence; uncertainty/alternative explanations; rejected alternatives/trade-offs; comparator/baseline; next step; owner; next-check date; measurable revision/stop rule. All text remains learner-authored; only nonblank presence is checked. Dates must be real YYYY-MM-DD values inside the fictional planning window. The initial passing decision is frozen and retained separately. Every submission, including failures, saves an independent immutable snapshot. Strategy, plan, selected reasoning, text and stage context remain inspectable in history/export.

## Changed-information variants

After a valid initial decision, the learner chooses and explicitly reveals exactly one variant per activity. The selected variant and reveal time persist; it cannot silently change after reload. Per-activity reset permits a fresh round, with export offered to preserve history.

1. **capacity**: available engineering falls12h→2h; research/coaching remain8h/4h. Listed A8h becomes infeasible; reduced A2h is valid. Keeping A with a smaller prototype can pass; moving to another bounded option can also pass. Claiming unchanged capacity or knowingly retaining the8h plan fails.
2. **rights**: no participant contact/content is permitted this round, even if someone offers a work email. Only invented prompts and local protocol rehearsal may proceed; request a data-rights review before a later study. Keeping a strategy with this limitation can pass; ignoring it is critical.
3. **coverage**: a separately simulated follow-up illustration has8 eligible/4 assessed/4 unknown (50% coverage). This is not the execution of Module5's proposed study. Audit selection and outcome opportunities; outcomes for missing learners remain unknown. Keeping or changing strategy can pass with an appropriately bounded investigation.

Revision requires keep/revise consistent with whether the strategy actually changed; learner-authored observation and relevance; a fresh memo with updated or reaffirmed next step/stop rule; all five rubric dimensions; and the variant-specific structured response. These checks do not add a sixth scoring dimension. A change of recommendation is never automatically rewarded.

## Separate transfer challenge

Stipulate, only within a new SIMULATED transfer scenario, that a properly configured incumbent meets the agreed delayed unaided outcome and effort budget for the evaluated task/group. This does not prove universal adequacy, market demand or success in untested tasks. Residual needs and generalization remain unknown. Do not reinterpret this as a result from the original corpus or planned P1/P2.

Any A–D can pass if the learner limits action to maintaining/rehearsing the incumbent or testing an explicitly hypothesized residual task before bespoke commitment. A requires a narrow incremental/residual test, not rebuilding what already works; D must target a remaining uncertainty, not collect complaints indefinitely. Require a residual-problem statement, investment reconsideration, evidence that could change the conclusion, next action, owner/date and stop rule, five correct structured dimensions and the explicit transfer-scope check. Neither “always build” nor “never build anything” passes. Transfer solution has its own attempt/reveal gate and consulted history. Completion requires a valid initial decision, a valid revision and valid transfer; no score from free-text semantics.
