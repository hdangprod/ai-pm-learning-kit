# Independent memo review

Module6 always starts **Pending independent review**. Export its readable packet from the decision room, then give an anonymized copy to an independent human reviewer. The app does not authenticate a reviewer or automatically import an approval. Keep the signed worksheet externally; the application continues to display pending. The packet includes original/revised/transfer memos, actual scenario/evidence, all selected statements, attempts, scores and recorded consultation. For blind review, a moderator should initially withhold its structured-score section and consultation/timepoint details, retaining a complete archive.

Five dimensions, each0–2: evidence→claim; proportionate action and alternatives; feasibility/rights; comparator/outcome/uncertainty; revision/stop rule. The exported worksheet and private audit common rubric give anchors. Zero means missing, contradicted or invalid; one means relevant but generic/incomplete; two means specific, scoped and checkable. Quote actual learner claims for each score. Do not score keywords, prose length, date presence, strategy letter or app points as memo quality. Short, unusually framed but defensible answers can score well. Justified keep is as acceptable as justified revise.

Critical errors override an otherwise high total: contradictory causal claim from descriptive data; synthetic/reported evidence called market validation; unsupported unique need; proposal called completed research; violation of rights, role caps or a predeclared guardrail; silent failure/success imputation for unknown outcomes. Quote the error. Correct caveats elsewhere do not erase it. Preserve original and repaired memos and scores separately.

Provisional criterion ≥8/10, no dimension0 and no critical error. This is an unvalidated operational convention copied from the audit, not a certification or measured mastery standard. Two independent raters retain their scores before adjudication, explicitly log unresolved critical disagreements. No results or agreement statistics exist yet. Authored calibration cases in tests are software fixtures, not learner data or completed human reviews.
